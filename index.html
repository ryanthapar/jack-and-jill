Good to know

Is Jack & Jill in-person or online?

Fully online. Live Zoom classes run twice a month, every Monday at 11 AM, so you can join from anywhere.

What's the difference between Jack & Jill and Humpty Dumpty?

Jack & Jill is the entry-level, online program for new and returning agents. Humpty Dumpty is built for more seasoned agents making $150K–$200K+, and runs in person for two hours a week with two additional flash calls per month.

What do I actually get?

Live Zoom coaching twice a month, activity reporting (door knocking, calls, and more), help setting your income goal, and biweekly reports to keep you accountable and consistent.

What's the commitment?

$149/month on a 12-month commitment — enough time to actually build the habits that stick, not just learn about them.

Who leads the program?

Jack & Jill is run by Ricardo Peixoto, Monica Thapar's right-hand man of 17 years. Enroll today and you'll also get a 1-on-1 branding session with Monica herself — a $1,500 value.
