FAQ

Good to know

Is Jack & Jill in-person or online?

Fully online. Live sessions run every Monday and Wednesday, so you can join from anywhere.

What's the difference between Jack & Jill and Humpty Dumpty?

Jack & Jill is the entry-level, online program for new and returning agents. Humpty Dumpty is built for more seasoned agents making $150K–$200K+, and runs in person for two hours a week with two additional flash calls per month.

What do I actually get?

Accountability, mindset, and consistency coaching, plus ongoing support for staying in front of your farm and a place to bring your real day-to-day questions.

Does the support end when the course does?

No. You get a full year of ongoing helpline support after the program, so you have somewhere to go when real questions come up.

Who leads the program?

Jack & Jill is led by Ricardo, with a focus on keeping things practical and hands-on.
